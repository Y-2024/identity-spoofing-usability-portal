Participant Information

Your participation is part of a research usability evaluation of a machine learning-based identity spoofing detection system.

Please follow the instructions provided by the researcher during the test.

Data Privacy and Confidentiality

Any audio and/or visual data provided during this study will be anonymized and used for research purposes only.

Survey responses are collected through Google Forms and may be stored in a linked Google Sheet configured by the researcher. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. The researcher should restrict access to the response sheet and use responses for this study only.

How to Use the System

  1. Select the test method requested by the researcher.
  2. Provide the required audio, image, or both.
  3. Review your input and click Run Spoof Detection.
  4. Wait for the detection result to appear.
  5. Follow the researcher's instructions before proceeding to the next task.

Important: There are no right or wrong answers during this usability test. Please perform each task as naturally as possible.

Usability Tasks

Task 1 — Audio
Provide an audio sample using the method requested by the researcher and run the detection.

Task 2 — Visual
Provide an image using the method requested by the researcher and run the detection.

Task 3 — Audio + Visual
Provide both an audio sample and an image, then run the detection.
